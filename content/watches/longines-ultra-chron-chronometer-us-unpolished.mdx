    Longines introduced calibre 431 in 1967 for the brand's 100th anniversary, in the middle of the "high-frequency wars" of the late 1960s. Beating at 36,000 vibrations per hour, it let Longines guarantee accuracy of around two seconds a day — one of the most precise series-made movements of its time, before quartz arrived. US-market Ultra-Chrons from the turn of the 60s and 70s were also sold with chronometer certification that emphasised exactly this exceptional precision.

S frekvenciou 36 000 kmitov za hodinu garantoval Longines presnosť okolo dvoch sekúnd za deň — jeden z najpresnejších sériovo vyrábaných strojčekov svojej doby, ešte pred nástupom quartzu. Americké trhové verzie Ultra-Chronu z prelomu 60. a 70. rokov sa predávali aj s chronometer certifikáciou, ktorá zdôrazňovala práve túto mimoriadnu presnosť.
