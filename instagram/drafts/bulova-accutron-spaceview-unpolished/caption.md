Bulova Accutron Spaceview, 1969

There's no dial on this one. You look straight at the movement. The calibre 214 tuning fork vibrates 360 times a second, so it doesn't tick, it hums, and the seconds hand glides instead of jumping. Bulova first built the Spaceview as a display piece for jewellers' windows. People wanted to wear it, and it became one of the best-known Accutrons.

This one's steel, 35 mm and never polished. I had it serviced in 2025, and it's a full set with box and papers, still on its original strap with the signed Bulova buckle.
