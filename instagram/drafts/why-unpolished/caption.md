Every watch I sell comes unpolished. If you want some shine back, that's your call.

A heavy polish takes metal off the case and with it the edges the maker cut. That can't be undone. A light polish for hairline scratches is different: it takes off as little as possible and keeps the case lines sharp. I offer it on request, before the watch ships or any time later.
