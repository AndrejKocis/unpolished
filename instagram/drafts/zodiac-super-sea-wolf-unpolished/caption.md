Zodiac Super Sea Wolf "75 ATM", early 1970s

Black dial, orange minute hand, rated to 750 m. Zodiac's patented crown and a redesigned caseback took the Sea Wolf from 200 m to 750 m, and that's how it ended up on the wrists of US Navy SEALs in the 70s.

This one's steel, 40 mm, automatic calibre 70-72, never polished and serviced in 2025. It's a full set with box and papers, on a period ZRC dive bracelet.
