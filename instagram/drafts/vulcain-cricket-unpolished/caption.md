This watch has an alarm, and it chirps like a cricket.

It's a Vulcain Cricket from around 1970, a vintage alarm watch that still works the way it did then. You set the alarm with the second crown, and the holes in the caseback carry the sound, so you'll actually hear it. Inside there's the hand-wound MSR S2, the successor to the famous calibre 120. Harry Truman got a Cricket in 1953, that's why people call it the presidents' watch.

It's 36 mm, gold-plated with a steel caseback and a silver sunburst dial, and I haven't polished the case. I had it serviced in 2026. Watch only, on a new green croc strap.
