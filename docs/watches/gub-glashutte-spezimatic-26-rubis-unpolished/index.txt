1d:T41b,East German classic straight out of Glashütte. Dial signed "Glashütte Spezimatic" and "26 Rubis", applied indexes and a date window. The gold dial carries an even patina earned with age — nothing redone, no refinish. The case is unpolished, Goldplaqué with a stainless steel caseback (Edelstahlboden) — the typical GUB build of this class, pairing gold plating with a tougher steel back. The caseback is engraved "GUB", "Stossgesichert" and "Made in GDR" along with the serial number. Currently on a leather strap.

<DidYouKnow logo="/watches/26-Rubis/logo.svg">
GUB — Glashütter Uhrenbetrieb — was the East German state watch company that, after the Second World War, merged the traditional Saxon workshops of Glashütte. Spezimatic was GUB's flagship automatic line of the 1960s and 70s, built on in-house movements developed without Swiss licences. The 26-jewel version is one of the better-equipped variants of the range and today makes an interesting, affordable entry into German — not Swiss — watchmaking history.
