1d:T42a,Tudor Oyster Royal ref. 7984 from 1965 — the 52xxxx serial matches the production period. Original silver satin dial with a sunburst effect, chrome baton indexes and stick hands with no sign of restoration, Tudor Rose at twelve, "Tudor Oyster Royal" and "Shock-Resisting" crisp and legible. Unpolished case, lugs with their full original geometry; the bezel keeps its original factory polish — not a later touch-up. Original Rolex-signed crown, coin-edge caseback undamaged. Hand-wound calibre 1156. Watch only — no box or papers, currently on a non-original leather strap.

<DidYouKnow logo="/watches/7984/logo.svg">
The Oyster Royal was the entry point to Tudor's Oyster family, which the brand built on cases supplied directly by Rolex. Reference 7984 from the mid-1960s has no date window — a simple three-hand dial with no complications that bet on legibility and reliability rather than features. The same case robustness as the Rolex of the day, at a lower entry price, is why collectors and beginners alike seek these pieces out today.
</DidYouKnow>
